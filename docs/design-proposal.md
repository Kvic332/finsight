# Design simplification proposal (for approval)

Nothing here is built yet. Approve, change or reject each item.

## Goal
Make the app answer three questions quickly: **How am I doing until payday? What should I do with my money? Is this provider safe?**

## Proposed changes
1. **Home shows the Payday plan.** Move "Left to spend freely" and the split (debts, budgets, savings) into a Home card, so the plan is seen daily, not hidden in Plan › Payday.
2. **Plan keeps three tabs:** Budget, Savings, Debts. Payday moves to Home.
3. **Quieter gamification.** Remove XP, levels ("Hustler", "Sharp Guy") and achievements from Insights' main view; keep the streak as a small line. Move achievements to a "Your progress" page in Settings.
4. **One "Needs your attention" card on Home**, combining new bank transactions, items to review and due debts, instead of separate cards.
5. **Insights leads with "Where your money went"**, with FinScore below it and a one-line reason for each score part.

## Not changing
Navigation tabs (Home, Activity, Plan, Grow), colours, fonts and Compare & Grow.
