How it works

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

FinSight works from the alerts your bank already sends to your phone. You never give it a bank login, and it never moves your money.

The three steps

01

Allow bank alerts

Turn on alert reading once in Settings. FinSight explains exactly what it reads before Android asks you. It only looks at notifications from bank and money apps, and SMS from bank sender names. Everything else is ignored and never kept.
