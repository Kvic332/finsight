FAQ

Good to know.

Short answers to what people ask most. Can't find yours? It will be answered here before launch.

Do I need to give FinSight my bank login?

No. FinSight never asks for bank logins, PINs or card details. It works from the alerts your bank already sends to your phone.

Which banks does it work with?

Alerts from over 25 Nigerian banks and money apps, including GTBank, Access, Zenith, First Bank, UBA, OPay, PalmPay, Kuda and Moniepoint, plus dollar accounts like Grey and Raenest. If yours is missing, you can still add transactions yourself or by voice.

Can FinSight move or spend my money?

No. FinSight can only read and organise information. It has no way to make payments or transfers.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. FinSight launches on Android first, because reading bank alerts on the phone is only possible there.

What happens if I change phones?

Turn on backups in Settings before you switch, then sign in on the new phone to restore. Without a backup, your data stays only on the old phone.

Does it work if my income changes every month?

Yes. Choose "varies" as your pay schedule, or "just tracking" if you don't want to set up income at all. Your FinScore stays fair either way.

I run a business. Will it mix my sales with my personal spending?

No. Business categories keep business money apart, so your personal budgets only count personal spending. You can see profit and pay yourself from the business.
