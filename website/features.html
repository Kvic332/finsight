Features

Everything to stay ahead of the month.

Built for how money really moves in Nigeria: transfers, POS, cash, side businesses, and salaries that don't always land on the same day.

All features

A plan for every pay

Paid monthly, twice a month or when it varies? FinSight works out what's free to spend after debts, bills and savings, and what that means each day.

Budgets that keep up

Set a limit for food, transport or data. Spending from your alerts counts against it straight away, with a heads-up before you run out.

Savings and debts

Track goals like rent or a new laptop, and loans with a repayment calculator that shows the real cost, reducing or flat interest.

Ask ARIA, in your language

ARIA is FinSight's money assistant. Ask anything about your spending and get a straight answer based on your own numbers.

Business and personal, apart

See profit, pay yourself, record cash sales, track who owes you and set money aside for tax, without mixing it with personal spending.

Dollars and pounds too

Paid through Grey, Raenest, Payoneer or Wise? Each amount keeps its own currency with that day's rate, or your own rate.

Your FinScore

One number for how your money is doing, with a plain explanation of what moves it. Fair even if your income isn't regular.

Import a statement

Bring in a bank statement and FinSight sorts the past few months, so your budgets start with real numbers.

Reminders that help

Bills coming up, money people owe you, and a short weekly summary of how the week went.

Say it, it's added

Paid cash? Tap the microphone and say what you spent. FinSight fills it in for you to check.

Made for how you earn

Salary, side hustle or both.

Tell FinSight how you use your money and it shapes Home around you.
