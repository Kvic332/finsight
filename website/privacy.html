Privacy

Your money data stays on your phone.

FinSight never asks for your bank login, can't move your money and doesn't show ads. Here's how your information is handled, in plain words. The full privacy policy will be published here before launch.

In short

Your money data stays on your phone

Transactions, budgets and goals are stored on your phone, encrypted.

Bank alerts never leave it

Only if you allow it, and only from bank and money apps. Alert text is read on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Backups only if you turn them on

With an account, a copy is kept with Google Firebase so you can restore it on another phone.

AI only when you use it

Your ARIA question and a summary of your finances, or a statement you import, go to Anthropic to get the answer. Not kept by FinSight, and not used to train AI.

Crash reports without personal details

Amounts, names and transactions are removed before a report is sent to Sentry.

Never sold, no ads

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with lenders. FinSight can't move your money.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, we keep your email address only to tell you when FinSight is available. We don't share it, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything from inside the app. See how to delete your account.
